## Critical Anti-Patterns (do NOT repeat these)
- **Per-page extraction without cross-page entity reconciliation** → produces dangling edges that get papered over with `Unknown`-kind stubs. Mitigation: pass already-extracted node_ids as context into subsequent page calls, or run a post-pass to drop edges with stub endpoints rather than synthesize stub nodes.
