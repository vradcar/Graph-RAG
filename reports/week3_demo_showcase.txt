Query #4: What is the modern replacement for the RTH6580WF?
============================================================================

  WHY GRAPH WINS HERE:
    Graph follows the REPLACED_BY chain (RTH6580WF → T9) and at depth=2 also
    surfaces what the T9 supports — the full migration picture. Vector has
    no way to traverse a replacement chain; it can only find documents that
    happen to mention both products together.

  --- GRAPH MODE ---
    (no graph evidence)

  --- VECTOR MODE ---
    - doc-1: T6-PRO is the modern replacement for legacy thermostat TH1110D in standard residential HVAC systems.
    - doc-2: WALL-PLATE-A is compatible with TH1110D and T6-PRO for retrofit installations.
    - doc-4: FAA and EASA commonly rely on aligned certification evidence for avionics software where DO-178C applies.
